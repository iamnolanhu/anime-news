Pitch (3 lines)

Shot Director is an open-source Cinema Studio: one frame in, a directed multi-shot sequence out.

Pick a camera body and a move; it writes the cinematography prompt and renders it with LTX 2.5 on Comfy Router.

MIT, one command to run, and every preset is plain text anyone can fork.
